Privacy Policy

TIC TAC TOE 999+

Effective date: 9 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TIC TAC TOE 999+ (“the App”), published under the QAVIB name, handles information when you use the game.

1. Information handled by the App

The App is a game with offline play, AI opponents and local Wi-Fi multiplayer features. You do not need to provide your name, phone number or email address to play the basic game.

The App may save game progress, scores, coins, XP, achievements and settings on your device so that your game can continue later. Local Wi-Fi multiplayer may use information needed to find or connect devices on the same local network. This local connection is used for gameplay and is not intended to publish your personal information on the internet.

2. Advertising

The App may offer optional rewarded video advertisements. If advertising is enabled in your version of the App, an advertising provider such as Google AdMob may process information such as your IP address, device or advertising identifiers, approximate device information, and ad interaction data to deliver, measure and help prevent fraud in advertisements. Watching a rewarded ad may provide in-game coins as shown in the App.

Google explains its data practices here: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

3. Sharing of information

QAVIB does not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by third-party services that provide advertising, if those services are enabled in the App. The providers' own privacy policies apply to their processing.

4. Data storage and security

Game progress and settings may be stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the App or clearing its storage may delete locally saved progress. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, but reasonable care should be taken to protect information handled by the App.

5. Children's privacy

The App is not designed to ask children to submit personal contact details. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information to QAVIB, contact us using the email below so we can review the concern.

6. Your choices

You can stop using the App at any time. You can manage or reset locally stored game data through your device's app settings, where available. You can also choose not to watch optional rewarded advertisements.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the App's features or data practices change. The latest version will be made available at the page where this policy is published. Please review it periodically.
